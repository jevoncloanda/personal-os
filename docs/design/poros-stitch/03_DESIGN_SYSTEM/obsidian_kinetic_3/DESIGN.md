## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive-tier, hyper-focused operating environment engineered for high-agency individuals managing wealth, cognitive workflow, and daily operational rhythm. It blends high-performance productivity tooling with the refined precision of institutional fintech.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Dark/OLED Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Luminescence**. Visual weight is anchored in deep midnight tones to minimize cognitive fatigue and maximize battery conservation on modern OLED displays, while high-velocity decisions are steered by focused Electric Indigo signals and razor-sharp micro-borders.

Key Brand Pillars:
- **Zero-Latency Clarity:** Every element conveys purpose instantly; visual hierarchy strictly dictates attention priority.
- **Sovereign Control:** Evoking autonomy, cryptographic security, and absolute precision.
- **Luminous Depth:** Elevating flat dark modes through measured, physics-based indigo light spills, subtle glass rims, and layered obsidian strata.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an OLED-first deep chromatic hierarchy, pairing obsidian slate ground tones with electric photonic accents.

### Ground & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`):** True midnight obsidian; establishes maximum contrast for text while remaining softer than absolute `#000000` to prevent chromatic aberration.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0F172A`):** Recessed wells, nested navigation rails, and backdrop fills.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#161F30`):** Default card resting tier and list container fill.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#1E293B`):** Elevated overlays, sheets, sticky headers, and active state wells.
- **Surface Level 4 (`#243048`):** High-interaction popovers, dialogs, and highlighted actionable cards.

### Accents & Emissive Tones
- **Primary Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Primary interactions, active tabs, and mission-critical metrics.
- **Electric Flare (`#818CF8`):** Hover states, icon highlights, and glowing strokes.
- **Deep Indigo / Foundation (`#4F46E5`):** Pressed button surfaces, selected pill backgrounds, and energetic structural dividers.
- **Indigo Aura (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`): Atmospheric ambient glows, card state shifts, and primary button hover envelopes.

### Typography & Border Hierarchy
- **Text Dominant (`#F8FAFC`):** Display headers, titles, high-value metrics, and active label text.
- **Text Subdued (`#94A3B8`):** Body copy, secondary metadata, inactive iconography, and captions.
- **Text Ghost (`#64748B`):** Placeholder labels, disabled states, and timestamps.
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Standard boundary hairline separating card elevations.
- **Border Structural (`#334155`):** Segmented dividers, active outlines, and focused control boundaries.

### Semantic Accents
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Positive delta, complete milestones, synced status.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Pending approvals, budget thresholds, attention items.
- **Danger (`#F43F5E`):** Destructive actions, system exceptions, capital outflow alerts.

## Typography

The typography system is constructed around a deliberate triad: **Sora** brings architectural authority and geometric precision to titles and primary metrics; **Hanken Grotesk** maintains neutral legibility for extended text and operational feeds; **JetBrains Mono** anchors financial balances, timestamp feeds, tags, and system telemetry with technical credibility.

### Usage Standards
- **Financial & Operational Figures:** All numbers, financial summaries, and system timecodes must use `JetBrains Mono` with tabular numeral formatting (`tnum`) to prevent jitter across dynamic updates.
- **Section Headers:** Employ `headline-md` or `headline-sm` with tight negative tracking to maintain density.
- **Text Hierarchies:** High contrast is strictly maintained: headers consistently leverage `#F8FAFC`, secondary descriptors default to `#94A3B8`, and monospaced telemetry items drop to `#64748B` unless actively signaling system status.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a rigid 4px base rhythm designed for native touch target accuracy and ergonomic thumb reach in mobile-first viewport contexts.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid canvas with a strict `margin-mobile` of `1rem` (16px). Grids inside cards scale down to 2 or 4 sub-columns with `gutter-sm` (12px).
- **Tablet / Split Screen (640px - 1024px):** Dual-pane responsive layouts leveraging an 8-column system with `gutter` (16px) and `margin-tablet` (24px).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Constrained 12-column layout maxing out at 1280px canvas container, anchored with `margin-desktop` (40px) and `gutter-lg` (24px).

### Spacing Usage
- `space-xs` (4px): Icon-to-text inline locks and micro-tag padding.
- `space-sm` (8px): Stacked related metadata items and vertical form gaps.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal padding for interactive cards, list item gutters, and container margins.
- `space-lg` (24px): Spatial distinction between disparate content widgets or card modules.
- `space-xl` (36px): Screen sections, bottom-sheet header offsets, and top safe-area boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural color strata and optical luminance rather than diffuse multi-color dropshadows. Surfaces build up from dark to light as they advance toward the user.

### Tiers of Physical Stacking
- **Floor Zero (Canvas):** `#0B0F19` — The deepest structural base layer.
- **Tier 1 (Recessed Containers):** `#0F172A` paired with an inner border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Standard Elevated Cards):** `#161F30` outlined with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Shadows are suppressed in favor of hairline illumination.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Menus & Trays):** `#1E293B` rimmed with `#334155`. Accompanied by a deep ambient occlusion shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Tier 4 (Modals & Command Center):** `#243048` surrounded by an ultra-crisp border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` and a soft Indigo backlight: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`.

### Glass & Translucency
Navigation bars and floating control pills use a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over `#0B0F19D9` (85% opacity), trimmed with a top-lit hairline border `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.12) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.02) 100%)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified structural profile balancing modern soft-cornered touch hardware with precision mechanics.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Default Geometry (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Inputs, interactive pill chips, nested sub-items, and form controls.
- **Large Cards & Drawers (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Primary functional cards, modular widgets, metrics tiles, and toast panels.
- **Modals & Action Sheets (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Bottom sheets, system prompts, command palettes, and floating app navigation bars.
- **Status Indicators & Micro-Tokens (`full` / 9999px):** Live indicator dots, counter badges, and biometric prompt triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#6366F1`, text `#F8FAFC`, subtle inner top highlight (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). In active/pressed states, color transitions to `#4F46E5` with a contained glow (`0 0 16px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Surface Action:** Background `#1E293B`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`. Pressed state shifts to `#243048`.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent fill, text `#94A3B8`, hovering or active state evokes `#161F30` with text `#F8FAFC`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Constructed with full pill radii.
- **Active Filter Chip:** Fill `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`, text `#818CF8`, border `1px solid #6366F1`.
- **Telemetry / Status Badge:** Fill `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #334155`, text `#94A3B8` in `label-sm`. Integrated dot uses semantic colors (Emerald `#10B981` with matching `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` drop shadow).

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Fill `#0F172A`, height 48px, radius 8px, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Typography:** Value in `Hanken Grotesk` 14px `#F8FAFC`; placeholder in `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions cleanly to `#6366F1` with an ambient glow spread of `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Module Panels
- Base fill `#161F30`, internal padding `16px`, corner radius `16px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Enclosed with a `1px` perimeter border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Critical cards (e.g., net worth, quick actions) apply a subtle radial gradient wash across the top-right corner using `radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08), transparent 70%)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size 20px x 20px, radius 6px for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unselected: Fill `#0F172A`, border `1.5px solid #334155`.
- Selected: Fill `#6366F1`, border `#6366F1`, with a high-contrast `#F8FAFC` checkmark icon.

### Operational Lists
- Separated via border hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) or discrete nested row surfaces (`#161F30`) with `4px` vertical spacing gaps.
- Leading icon containers sit on a `#1E293B` square pad with 8px radius and `1px` subtle outline.
- Trailing quantitative information strictly displayed via `JetBrains Mono` in `label-md` or `label-lg`.

### Quick-Access Floating Dock (Mobile Navigation)
- Elevated dock pinned 16px above the device home indicator.
- Surface `#0F172AD9` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, rounded `24px` (`rounded-xl`), bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Active item displays an Electric Indigo micro-pill highlight beneath the icon with `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)` backlighting.
